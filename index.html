About Ozen
She considers the other White Whistles less friendly than her, especially Bondrewd.
Her design is based on that of witches and the Minotaur from Greek Mythology.
Ozen grew as tall due to the use of Artifacts. She owns more than just the Thousand-Men Pins.
She has a strangely shaped monochrome hairstyle that conceals scalp deformations caused by extensive exposure to The Curse of the Abyss.
Hey you. Do you know what "God" means? The people around here, you see, don't really believe in God. Instead, do you know what it is they believe in? It's this place. The Abyss itself. Because the bottom of the Abyss is unknown, and since they're afraid of it, it replaces their God.
